Our approach

Evidence-adjacent methods, delivered gently

Our practitioners are trained in modern conflict resolution and at least one version control system each.

✿

Three-way empathy

We always involve the common ancestor. You have both changed since then. That is allowed. That is growth. That is also the problem.

✿

Non-violent conflict markers

The markers are not walls. They are boundaries, and boundaries are healthy. Nobody is "less than", except in the diff, syntactically.

✿

Acceptance work

Sometimes both changes are valid. Sometimes neither is. Sometimes the healthiest resolution is deleting the file and sitting quietly.

Sessions

Formats of care

All sessions are 50 minutes, like a sprint that respects you.

Individual

You and your uncommitted feelings

For the developer holding changes they are afraid to push. We explore why. The answer is usually the reviewer.

50 min. Stash beforehand if needed.
Couples

HEAD and incoming

Our core practice. Two branches, one file, irreconcilable opinions about naming. We facilitate. The working tree is a safe space.

50 min. Both parties must be fetched.
Group

The monorepo circle

For teams whose conflicts have conflicts. Chairs are arranged in a directed acyclic graph. Everyone gets to speak, in topological order.

50 min. Attendance is eventually consistent.
What to expect

Your first session

  • Each branch reads its version aloud. No interrupting. No force-pushing while the other is speaking.
  • The therapist asks how that made HEAD feel. HEAD is encouraged to use "I" statements, such as "I am detached".
  • Together we identify what you are really fighting about. It is never the code. It is respect, recognition, and one comment from a review in 2024.
  • Homework is assigned. It is usually "talk to each other before opening the pull request". Nobody does it. We assign it anyway, gently.

We have availability

We always have availability. Conflicts are constant. It is the one thing we can promise you.

In crisis right now?

If you are currently in a detached HEAD state, do not attempt to resolve alone. Our crisis line is staffed around the clock by counsellors who will stay with you until you are safely on a named branch.

git checkout -b somewhere-safe
curl https://mergeconflictcounselling.com/crisis

We also make house calls. counsel, our mergetool, will sit with you and your conflict in the terminal, hear both sides in full, and leave the resolution where it belongs: with you. curl -fsS https://mergeconflictcounselling.com/counsel to bring the practice home.
